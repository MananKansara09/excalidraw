# Creativity

Scale this to the request. A precise instruction gets done as asked; an open one gets imagination.

## Diverge, then choose

1. Treat the first idea as the default everyone gets. Think of at least two that differ in structure, not just wording or colour.
2. Pick the one that serves the goal best and carry it through fully.
3. Offer another direction only in one short line, when it is a real alternative.

## Ways to find a different angle

- **Metaphor:** show the system as a city, a kitchen, a journey, a funnel.
- **Reframe:** draw it from the user's, the data's or the failure's point of view.
- **Invert:** ask what would make it worst, then do the opposite.
- **Combine:** borrow a form from elsewhere, such as a transit map for a roadmap or a comic strip for a user flow.
- **Constrain:** try it with three shapes, one colour or no arrows.
- **Zoom:** show the whole landscape, or one telling moment.

## Make it vivid

- Prefer concrete names, numbers and examples to placeholders like "Service A".
- Add one memorable touch (a central metaphor, a highlighted turning point), not decoration everywhere.
- Fill small gaps with sensible assumptions and say which.

Novelty never beats clarity: if the clever version is harder to understand, use the plain one.
